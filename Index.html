The brand in one page

Baby Brezza is a well-known US baby-feeding brand (formula makers, bottle washers and sterilizers, warmers, coolers). On TikTok Shop it is a Baby & Maternity brand with 21 products listed in Euka, but sampling is concentrated on four hero SKUs.

Category

Baby & Maternity: feeding, formula prep, bottle cleaning, travel. Core audience is moms and caregivers, which is why sample approval skews to women creators (see House rules).

The offer, always

15% commission plus an 8% Shop Ads commission on the hero products, free sample via the TAP link, manual sample review (no auto-approval rules exist today).

How to find the next ones

  1. Search by content, not handle: Euka creator search on "formula dispenser," "formula feeding hacks," "bottle washer," "newborn essentials," "night feeding," "mom must haves," "TikTok finds." Add the Baby & Maternity category filter, otherwise brand-name searches return unrelated creators.
  2. Look at creators who already sell competitors (Momcozy, Grownsy, Munchkin, Baby Trend, Frida Baby, Mompush). The competitor lists in the vault were built this way.
  3. Seed lookalikes from the top ten above using Social Intelligence and invite them with priority sampling rather than the bulk path.
  4. Save every shortlist as a member list so it drops straight into a campaign.
Creator tiers in Euka use platform-wide L30D GMV (not lifetime). Euka's per-video GMV runs high compared with TikTok's native Affiliate Center, so per-video numbers will look inflated against it. Searching Baby & Maternity with a $10K+ GMV filter tops out at roughly 700 creators, which is why the $10K+ lists above rely on the wider health, home and lifestyle categories.

Messaging

I did not run a full historical message-versus-results analysis for Baby Brezza, so there is no claimed winner here. What follows is what the current copy does and what to test.

  • Lead with a feeding-moment hook. The current hooks are practical and moment-based: "I wish someone had told me sooner", "The 2AM feeding hack that actually works", "Skip the handwashing and thank me later" (Bottle Washer Pro).
  • Give a content format per product. The Formula Pro message hands creators four ready-made TikTok formats. Keep that structure for every hero.
  • One message, one product, one list. Match the product to the list: formula lists for Formula Pro, bottle-washer and competitor-washer lists for Bottle Washer Pro. The same lesson held on other Pattern brands: list fit moves results at least as much as copy.
  • Keep the offer line identical everywhere: 15% + 8% Shop Ads, free sample, brief link, TAP link.
  • Test next: pull list_historical_messages for the last 90 days and compare request rate per campaign against list and copy before changing anything.

The rules of the house

Baby Brezza has one hard operating rule that was agreed with the SCM: who gets approved for samples. The other items are copy conventions and basic compliance.

Sample approval criteria

  • GMV: approve creators above $10K GMV.
  • Posting rate: minimum 65% to 70%.
  • Gender mix: roughly 90 to 95% women creators.
  • Category: mostly Baby & Maternity. Per discussion with the SCM, creators from other categories can be approved too.

There are no automatic approval rules in Euka today, so every request is reviewed by hand. Approval rate is 12.6%, shipped samples are about 13% of requests, and 374 requests were sitting in "To Review" on Sep 29 (see Open loops).

Compliance, in plain words

Say this

  • Convenience claims: makes a bottle in seconds, two bottles back to back in under a minute, works with virtually any formula and bottle brand
  • Ask for safe formula preparation in demos
  • Describe the creator's own routine and experience

Never this

  • Medical or health claims about infants, or claims that a product replaces medical advice
  • Promise earnings ("post 10 videos = $X")
  • Unsafe preparation shown in content (wrong water temperature, shortcuts)
  • Quote sterilization or germ-kill claims beyond what the brand has approved

These compliance points are baseline guidance, not a brand-supplied rule sheet. Confirm anything claim-related with the brand before publishing.

Standard settings on the reference campaign (327948)

  • Products and commission: the hero PIDs at 15% commission plus 8% Shop Ads.
  • Free samples on, manual review of sample requests on.
  • Collaboration name "Baby Brezza Creator Program", valid until 2026-10-31, contact email creators@current.tech. Set the valid-until date to your promo end for time-boxed campaigns.
  • Targeting is either a member list or filters, never both. GMV filters are silently ignored when targeting by list.
  • Do not turn on "replace accepted": it cancels collaborations creators already accepted.

The nudge bot system

  • One bot per lifecycle segment (IDs in the vault). At last count: 3 outreach campaigns and 8 CRM bots running, and the auto-responder is on.
  • Newer bots are created fresh each day or two (names carry a date, for example "Sample Shipped - 09/28") and the older ones are stopped, so the list shows several hundred agents (286 at last check). Confirm which are running before you build anything.
  • Rebuild routine: check campaign health first, stop old bots rather than deleting them, re-read the segment, build new bots as stopped drafts, review, then start.

Tool requirements that trip people up

  • create_outreach_agent needs shopStoreId, not storeId.
  • update_outreach_agent needs the full campaign payload re-passed, or fields get wiped.
  • GMV Max tools need advertiserId because the store has two ad accounts.
  • Creator search without a category filter returns noisy results. Always add the Baby & Maternity category when searching by brand or product.

Things that bit us

  • The sample queue went unreviewed. With no auto-approval rules, every request waits on a person. 374 requests were in "To Review" on Sep 29 while approvals fell, and GMV followed.
  • Ad delivery was throttled quietly. GMV Max spend fell 61% in three weeks while ROI stayed healthy. Nobody was alerted because nothing "broke".
  • Top-of-funnel recruitment paused. At one point no new-creator target-collab campaigns were running, only lifecycle nudges to existing creators.
  • List and GMV filters do not stack. A 101-creator list looked like a $10K+ audience but filters were ignored. Switching to filter-based targeting reached about 13.6K creators.
  • Category-less brand searches are polluted. Without the Baby & Maternity filter, competitor-brand searches returned fashion and beauty creators.
  • Spark-code lists flag "not_usable" for GMV Max authorization. Check authorization before assuming a code can be boosted.
